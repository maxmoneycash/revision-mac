The Mac app

Revision 0.1.1 processes screenshots and annotations on your Mac. It does not send them to an AI provider or our servers. It does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

A recovery copy of your current project is stored in ~/Library/Application Support/Revision/Recovery.revision. Files you explicitly save or export are stored where you choose. Your trial start date and activated license are stored in macOS Keychain.

Feedback bundles include your screenshots, annotations, and saved project. You decide whether to share these with another person or an AI tool; that service's privacy practices then apply.

This website

This static site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising cookies, or email collection. GitHub may process technical request data to deliver and secure its hosting service. Download and feedback links take you to GitHub.

Questions and deletion

You can remove saved projects, exported folders, and the recovery file using Finder. Trial and license entries use the service dev.maxmohammadi.revision.license in Keychain. For product questions, open a support issue. Do not post private screenshots or license keys in public issues.
